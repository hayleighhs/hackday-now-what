The big idea

See when you can afford each thing.

Budget spreadsheets are everywhere. What’s missing is the link between what you want and the money you’re putting aside for it.

You rank what you want. Your pot pays for things in that order, one at a time.

The dates work themselves out. Every item shows when it’s ready, based on what’s in your pot and what you add each month. Reorder the list and the dates change instantly.

Need it sooner? “Find me a way to get this now” shows interest-free options only: save a little more each month, use a 0% card wisely, or add it to your gift list for friends and family.

You stay in control. Nothing moves money automatically. When you buy something, you mark it as bought and it comes out of your pot.

What’s inside

Four tabs, one calm place.

It opens on a big, friendly welcome. Pick what’s next for you, then move around with four tabs at the bottom of the screen.

Rooms

A moodboard for every room, so you can see it coming together.

  • Paste a link, add a price and a photo
  • Tap through to the shop any time
  • No more lost tabs

Purchases

Everything you want, in the order you want it.

  • A ready date for every item
  • A gift list you can filter by price
  • Share it to WhatsApp in one tap

Budget

What comes in, what goes out, and what’s left.

  • Typical costs for a two-bed flat to start from
  • Mark each bill as an estimate or actual
  • Choose what goes in your furniture pot

Fixes

Chips, leaks and paint jobs, all in one list.

  • Jobs by room, plus a general list
  • Mark each one as to do, reported or done
  • Share outstanding fixes with your management company

How it’s designed

Warm, clear, and on your side.

Plain language first

Short sentences and everyday words. Clear matters more than clever, and nobody gets talked down to.

Private by default

Everything stays on your phone. No accounts, no sign-ups, nothing shared.

Guidance, not sales

No bank or retailer behind it. It never names credit products or pushes you to borrow.

Made for one hand

Designed for a phone first, with big tap targets and soft pastel colours.
